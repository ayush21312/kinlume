Privacy policy

Updated October 24, 2026

Draft for the current prototype. Operator and contact details are not finalized. Review and complete this document before public launch.

Scope and current status

This draft describes the current Kinlume prototype, not a future hosted version. It uses a local Node server and SQLite database. It does not currently use Supabase. Operator and public contact information are intentionally blank. Hosting and email providers must be added to this notice when connected.

Information you provide

Accounts use an email address and password. The server stores a salted password hash rather than a readable password. Your survey can contain your purpose, preferred name, assistance needs, search area, travel and accessibility preferences, optional household range and circumstances, and optional notes. Organization managers submit public organization and resource information. Donors submit item quantities; the app stores pledges and delivery records.

How information is used

Account information supports sign-in and ownership checks. Private survey answers seed searches and visit preferences. Organization records support public listings and donation requests. Pledge and delivery records support accurate totals. Organizations do not receive your private questionnaire through their dashboard. Only provide information needed for these functions.

AI messages and personalization

Chat messages, recent conversation context, selected resource facts, and visit preferences you explicitly discuss are sent through the server to the configured AI provider, currently Groq. If you opt in to saved-profile personalization, selected needs and travel preferences are included. Your name, account email, and private notes are excluded from automatic profile context. Anything you type into chat is part of that message, including sensitive details you choose to include. Provider processing and retention are governed by the provider’s terms; Kinlume does not promise zero provider retention.

Directory, location, map and photo services

Location and resource-type searches may be sent to Feed America (feedam.org), Give Food, Photon, or OpenStreetMap-related query providers depending on the region and available data. Resource names and locations may be sent to Wikimedia Commons to find licensed photos. Your browser requests map tiles and images from their servers, which can receive your IP address and ordinary request information. Selecting current location asks the browser for permission; map coordinates are processed in the current page rather than saved in your questionnaire automatically. Following directions, contact, source, or other external links shares ordinary browser request information with that destination.

What is public and what is private

Published organization/resource listings and supply requests are visible to other app users. Organization owners can review relevant pledge quantities and delivery records. Private surveys are account-owned. Do not put private personal information into public organization names, descriptions, addresses, requests, or uploaded listing content.

Cookies and device storage

A session cookie keeps you signed in and expires after seven days unless you sign out earlier. Browser localStorage keeps saved resource IDs and snapshots, your last search area, text-size preference, and any earlier device-only donation records. Saved places are not currently synced through your account. Chat transcripts and individual visit plans stay in the current React page session and are lost on reload. Clearing browser storage removes device records but does not delete server-side account records.

Retention and controls

Accounts, surveys, organization records, pledges, and deliveries remain in the local server database; automatic account deletion and a retention schedule are not implemented. Expired sessions are removed when a new session is created. You can edit preferences, disable saved-profile AI personalization, sign out, unsave places, and cancel eligible outstanding pledges. There is no self-service account export/deletion workflow or public contact channel yet. Those controls and a practical request process must be finalized before public launch.

Security and technical records

The app uses password hashing, server-side session checks, and organization ownership checks. These measures do not guarantee security. Application code does not intentionally log chat text, questionnaire answers, or API keys; provider error codes and routine server diagnostics may be logged. Hosting and external services may collect additional technical records under their own policies. Public hosting requires HTTPS, production access controls, and backups.

Advertising, children and policy changes

The current app code includes no advertising or analytics SDK and no feature for selling personal information. Third-party services still have their own practices. The prototype is not designed for children under 13 and has no parental-consent system; do not submit children’s identifying details. This is not a claim of universal legal compliance. Update this notice when data practices change, and complete the operator, contact and request process before public registration.

Public contact details: not provided.
