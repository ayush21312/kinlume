About Kinlume

Kinlume is a community-resource prototype. It helps people discover assistance, plan possible visits, and coordinate item donations. It is not an emergency service, government agency, or a guarantee of assistance. These draft terms describe the prototype and its intended use; operator and contact information have not been finalized.

Listings and visit plans

Directory records, hours, photos, eligibility information, and map positions can be incomplete or outdated. Confirm details directly with the organization before travelling. Recommendations do not establish eligibility, reserve supplies, make appointments, or arrange transportation. Estimated travel times are not live routes or transit schedules.

AI assistance

The assistant uses a third-party AI service with selected resource facts. Responses can contain mistakes. Do not rely on it for emergency response or professional medical, legal, or financial decisions. Do not enter identity documents, Social Security numbers, bank details, passwords, or other people’s private information.

Accounts and appropriate use

Use accurate information, protect your password, and manage only organizations you are authorized to represent. Do not impersonate a nonprofit, publish fraudulent requests, post unlawful or abusive content, scrape personal information, or try to bypass security or ownership checks. This prototype does not provide email verification, account-recovery email, or a parental-consent process. Do not create accounts for children under 13.

Organization listings

Organization managers are responsible for the accuracy of their own listings, contact details, services, and supply requests, and for having permission to publish supplied content. A listing does not mean Kinlume independently verified or endorsed an organization. Imported directory records remain subject to their original sources and cannot be edited by an arbitrary Kinlume account.

Item donations

A pledge expresses an intention to give items; it is not confirmation of delivery. Only the receiving organization records received items. Coordinate acceptance and drop-off directly with the organization. Kinlume does not process money, transport donations, issue tax receipts, or guarantee fulfillment.

Content and third-party services

Original Kinlume app content and code remain subject to their owners’ rights. Directory records, maps, photos, libraries, and linked websites retain their own ownership, licenses, and terms. Third-party names and logos do not imply partnership or endorsement. Users retain ownership of content they submit and allow it to be stored and displayed as needed to operate their listings and donation requests.

Availability and changes

The prototype may change or be unavailable. External providers may fail or limit requests. Current local storage is not a public hosting or backup service. No promise of uninterrupted operation, current inventory, or a successful visit is made. Any future finalized terms must accurately describe the deployed service; changes will be identified by an updated date.

Questions and contact

Public operator and contact details are intentionally blank while this is a private prototype. A working contact method and reviewed terms must be added before inviting public account registrations. No registered-company or registered-trademark status is claimed.
